Role-Based Workflows

A clear, professional breakdown of how each role interacts with the LOCUS system daily.

📦

Customer (The Receiver)

The end-user waiting for their package delivery.

Authentication

No app required. Customers do not need to download software or create passwords. All interactions occur via secure SMS links.

1

Receives SMS Notification

When their package is processed at the origin hub, they automatically receive an SMS containing a secure tracking link.

2

Live 3D Tracking

Clicking the link opens a public web interface where they can view the real-time location and status of their package on a 3D map.

3

Secure OTP Handover

When the delivery agent arrives, the customer receives a final SMS OTP (One-Time Password). They must provide this code to the agent to verify their identity and receive the package.

🛵

Delivery Partner

The last-mile agent completing the final drop-off.

Authentication

Logs into the mobile app using their phone number and an SMS OTP. The system recognizes their role and instantly loads their daily delivery manifest.

1

Route Optimization

The app groups nearby parcels together and provides an optimized map route, ensuring they visit addresses in the most efficient order.

2

Geofenced Drop-offs

To prevent false delivery claims, the "Mark as Delivered" button remains disabled until the phone's hardware GPS proves the agent is within 100 meters of the geocoded address.

3

OTP Verification

The agent asks the customer for their SMS OTP, enters it into the app, and finalizes the delivery. This mathematically proves the package reached the correct recipient.

🏢

Hub Manager

The administrator overseeing warehouse operations.

Authentication

Logs into the Web Dashboard on a computer using their phone number and an SMS OTP. Gains access to the Command Center based on their administrative permissions.

1

Generates Identifiers

Before operations begin, they use the Printer module to generate and print batches of blank QR codes for individual parcels (TRK) and transit bags (BAG) using thermal label printers.

2

Live Network Monitoring

They monitor a live topology map showing active trucks and shipments moving across the supply chain, ensuring regional operations remain on schedule.

3

Exception Handling

If the system detects anomalies (such as a compromised QR code or a misrouted shipment), the manager receives instant alerts in their Security Inbox to investigate.

👷

Warehouse Worker

The staff responsible for intake, sorting, and packing.

Authentication

Logs into the mobile app via SMS OTP. The app automatically provisions the specific scanning and consolidation tools needed for warehouse operations.

1

AI-Assisted Intake

Upon receiving a package, they affix a blank QR code and photograph the handwritten shipping bill. Mistral Vision AI instantly extracts the text, eliminating manual data entry.

2

Smart Consolidation

To prepare shipments for transit, they scan individual parcels and pack them into larger Transit Bags assigned to specific destination cities.

3

Routing Validation

If a worker attempts to pack a parcel into a bag destined for the wrong city, the application flashes red and blocks the scan, acting as a physical firewall against routing errors.

🚚

Line Driver

The driver transporting sealed transit bags between major hubs.

Authentication

Logs into the mobile app via SMS OTP. The app recognizes them as a transit driver and displays the bags assigned to their vehicle.

1

Departure Scan

They scan the sealed Transit Bag to register it as "In-Transit", load it into the truck, and depart for the destination hub.

2

Offline Resilience

If the truck travels through areas with poor cellular reception, the app seamlessly switches to Offline Mode, queuing scans locally and syncing them once a connection is reestablished.

3

Anti-Clone Speed Validation

The system continuously checks the distance and time between scans. If a QR code is scanned in two distant cities too quickly, it proves the code was cloned and instantly flags it for security review.
