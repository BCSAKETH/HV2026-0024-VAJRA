2. The Core Data Engine

How the backend constructs an unbreakable chain of custody.

Shipment & Bag

The physical items. Shipments are individual parcels that get packed into Bags. Bags are assigned to Hubs or Staff during transit.

Hub & PincodeRoute

The geographical backbone. Hubs own specific Pincode Routes. Staff are strictly assigned to specific Hubs.

TrackingEvent & SecurityEvent

The Immutable Ledger. Every scan logs a Tracking Event. If the system detects tampering (like teleportation speed), it escalates to a Security Event (e.g., COMPROMISED, AUTO_HEALED).

Staff & Role

Defines who did what. Every physical action requires an authenticated Staff ID, eliminating anonymous errors.

3. The 3D Rendering Engine

A modular approach to high-performance visual storytelling.

«abstract» Scene3D

Every 3D scene in LOCUS inherits from a master Scene3D class. This ensures that features like reduced-motion and low-power fallback are handled automatically across the entire app without rewriting code.

PackageBoxScene

A standalone visual that can open or sit idle. No external data dependencies.

JourneyPath3D

Derives its waypoints directly from the TrackingEvent ledger to draw the path.

HubSelector3D

Drives the DashboardScope, letting managers select a hub visually.

Component Contract

Domain types and 3D scenes are completely decoupled for maximum performance.
